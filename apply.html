Apply for SECP Certification

The process is designed to be straightforward. Experienced coaches can typically complete the application in a few hours and receive a decision within 1–2 weeks.

Application Overview

  • Experience summary (years, client level, volume)
  • 2–3 professional references (contact info)
  • 1–2 anonymized case summaries with outcomes
  • Short coaching philosophy statement (optional but helpful)
  • Signed ethics commitment
  • Proof of professional liability insurance

Estimated application fee: $495 (review + directory listing for first year)

Annual renewal: $295 (keeps directory listing active)

No multi-month training required. Experience is the primary criterion.

This is a prototype site for the Society of Executive Coaching Practitioners concept. To move forward with actual applications, incorporate the association, secure a domain, and implement a simple application form or intake process (email or form tool).
