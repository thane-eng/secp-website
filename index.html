Why SECP Exists

Experience Over Hours

Simple & Verifiable

When asked “Are you certified?” the answer is clear: “Yes, SECP Certified.” Buyers can verify status instantly via our public directory.

Practical Alternative

Designed for practitioners who have already proven their ability with senior leaders and organizations—without additional gatekeeping.

How It Works

1. Document Your Practice

Submit years of experience, client references, anonymized case outcomes, and a short ethics commitment.

2. Lean Review

Application review focused on evidence of practice. Possible short interview or case discussion—not a multi-day assessment.

3. Directory Listing

Once certified, appear in the public SECP directory. Buyers confirm your credential in seconds.
